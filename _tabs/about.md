# About Me

*Exploring ideas. Writing to learn. Learning through writing.*

Hi there — I’m Marlise 👋  

I currently work in accounting, but I’m also on a broader learning journey. This blog is my space to explore ideas, experiment with writing, and share what I’m learning along the way.  

I believe in learning by doing — and for me, writing is part of that process. I write to learn, and I learn through writing. Sometimes that means diving into technical topics, sometimes it’s about personal growth or new discoveries. It’s all part of connecting the dots.  

This blog isn’t about perfection, it’s about progress. If you’re curious, love exploring new ideas, or are also figuring things out one post at a time, then you’ll fit right in.
